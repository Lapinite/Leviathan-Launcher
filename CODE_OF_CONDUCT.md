# Leviathan Project Code of Conduct

**Last updated:** 8 September 2026

Leviathan project spaces should be useful, safe, and respectful for contributors, testers, users, and maintainers.

## Expected conduct

Participants should:

- communicate respectfully and focus criticism on ideas, code, behavior, or evidence;
- avoid harassment, threats, stalking, discrimination, or targeted humiliation;
- avoid sexual harassment or unwanted sexual content;
- respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information;
- avoid spam, scams, impersonation, malware, credential theft, or social engineering;
- follow project security-reporting procedures instead of publishing exploitable vulnerabilities irresponsibly;
- respect intellectual-property and platform rules;
- follow reasonable moderator or maintainer instructions intended to protect the project and community.

## Unacceptable conduct

The project may act on behavior that creates a credible safety, security, fraud, harassment, or disruption risk, including conduct outside project spaces when it directly affects the Leviathan community or infrastructure.

## Enforcement

Depending on severity and context, maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, reject contributions, suspend access to project-operated services, or permanently ban participation.

Serious security, fraud, abuse, or safety incidents may be escalated to relevant service providers or authorities where appropriate and lawful.

## Appeals

Where a public moderation system is later provided, material enforcement decisions should have a reasonable appeal path unless doing so would create a security or legal risk.

## Security reports

Do not publish secrets or active exploit details in a public issue. Follow `SECURITY.md`.
