# Leviathan Launcher Code of Conduct

Leviathan Launcher aims to maintain a respectful, constructive, and safe project community.

This Code of Conduct applies to project-controlled community spaces when they are made available, including GitHub discussions, issue trackers, contributor spaces, support channels, and other official Leviathan Launcher community areas.

---

## Expected Behavior

Participants should:

- Be respectful
- Communicate constructively
- Assume good faith where reasonable
- Respect privacy
- Give useful technical feedback
- Avoid unnecessary hostility
- Follow project rules
- Respect third-party rights
- Report security issues responsibly

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following may result in moderation:

- Harassment
- Threats
- Hate speech
- Targeted abuse
- Doxxing
- Publishing private personal information
- Sexual harassment
- Persistent disruptive behavior
- Spam
- Scams
- Phishing
- Malware
- Credential theft
- Account theft
- Piracy assistance
- Ownership or authentication bypass assistance
- Deliberate security abuse
- Impersonation of project staff
- Misrepresentation of unofficial builds as official

---

## Security Disclosures

Do not publicly post:

- Passwords
- Access tokens
- Refresh tokens
- Session cookies
- Private keys
- Client secrets
- Exploit details that create an immediate risk to users

Security vulnerabilities should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

---

## Enforcement

The project maintainer may remove, edit, hide, lock, or reject content that violates this Code of Conduct.

Access to project-controlled spaces may be restricted for serious or repeated violations.

Enforcement decisions may consider:

- Severity
- Intent
- Repetition
- Security impact
- Harm to users
- Disruption to the project

---

## Scope

This policy applies to official Leviathan Launcher spaces.

It does not claim authority over unrelated third-party communities, though behavior outside official spaces may be considered where it creates a direct safety or security risk to the project or its users.
